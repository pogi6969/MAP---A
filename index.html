Know the route, the time and the fare before you ride.

MAP-A plans door-to-door trips on jeepneys, UV Express vans, buses and on foot across all 18 cities and municipalities of Albay. It prices every leg with the LTFRB fare matrix, shows what you can reach in 30 minutes, and finds the nearest hospital that can take a patient.

Features

One app for the trip, the fare and the hospital

Every screen below comes from the working MAP-A prototype.

Directions

Door-to-door directions with the fare

Pick a start and an end. MAP-A tells you when you will arrive, how much you will pay, which vehicle to take, and where to get on and off.

  • Fastest, shortest, fewest transfers or cheapest
  • Regular, Student, Senior and PWD fares with the 20 percent discount
  • Walking directions for the first and last mile, with a cap on walking distance
  • Light, typical or heavy traffic

Bicol University to Sumlang Lake with the student discount.

Routes and fares

Every route, with how often it runs and what it costs

The app carries 30 services: city jeepney loops, provincial jeepneys, UV Express vans, buses and a sea crossing. Each one lists where it runs, how often, its operating hours and its fare.

  • A fare lookup between any two named landmarks
  • Each leg is charged separately, so a transfer means a new minimum fare

Fares shown with the student discount.

Thirty-minute reach

What each town can reach by public transport

Opportunity access shows the share of the province's hospital beds, school seats and jobs that each town can reach by public transport within the time budget. Slide the budget from 15 to 120 minutes.

  • A population-weighted Gini coefficient for how evenly access is shared
  • Colored circles mark which towns are well served and which are not

Budget set to 30 minutes.

Vehicle classes

Roads that fit the vehicle

Choose from ten vehicle classes, from a pedestrian to a provincial bus. Roads that are too narrow, too low or too weak for the class are left out and marked, so a truck or an ambulance gets a road it can use.

  • Width, height, gross mass and axle load are checked on every road
  • Meant to help route relief trucks during typhoons and Mayon alerts

A 10-wheel truck from Legazpi Grand Central Terminal to Ligao City. Red dashes mark restricted roads.

Hospital mode

The nearest hospital that can take the patient

One switch hides every landmark except hospitals and infirmaries. MAP-A then looks for the closest one with the right level of care, an open emergency room when it is needed, a bed that is not reported full, and a cost the family can carry.

  • Two travel modes: an ambulance on the road network, or the patient on public transport and on foot
  • Five patient-status levels in plain language, chosen by the crew or the user
  • Four payment choices, from free or assisted care only to any hospital
  • For the two most urgent levels the cost filter is lifted, in line with Republic Act No. 10932, which bars hospitals from asking for a deposit before emergency care
  • Each result shows travel time, care level, cost class and free beds with the age of the report
  • A hospital that has not reported is shown as unknown, never as available
  • Stabilize first: when an open emergency room is much nearer than the right hospital, the app says so
  • A one-tap call to 911 on every hospital screen

Navigation support, not triage.Hospital mode does not assess the patient and does not replace 911 or the judgment of an ambulance crew. In an emergency, call 911.

How bed counts would reach the app

Hospital registers list licensed beds, not free ones. A small network of e-mail agents asks each hospital for its numbers on a fixed schedule.

  1. Coordinator

    Opens a cycle

    Every 8 hours, one per nursing shift, or once a day if the hospital prefers.

  2. Inquiry

    Sends the question

    One agent per district e-mails each hospital a short form with a one-time code.

  3. Reader

    Reads the replies

    Checks the mailbox every 10 minutes and reads English or Filipino. Anything unclear goes to a person.

  4. Validator

    Runs five checks

    Sender and code, completeness, capacity, occupancy and recency.

  5. Status

    Shows free beds

    The app shows free beds with the time of the report.

How it works

From open data to a trip plan, in eight stages

No field survey and no volunteers. Every input comes from a public register or from the rules the study sets out.

  1. Stage 1

    Road network

    Roads and paths from OpenStreetMap, with width, height, mass and axle limits where known.

  2. Stage 2

    Landmarks

    Terminals, town halls, markets, schools and hospitals from public registers, each tied to its nearest road node.

  3. Stage 3

    Transit feed

    Stops, routes, trips, fares and shapes written as a GTFS feed and checked with the open GTFS validator.

  4. Stage 4

    Timetable graph

    A time-expanded graph answers earliest-arrival queries for every landmark and departure time.

  5. Stage 5

    Access and fairness

    Thirty-minute access for each landmark, summed up with the Gini and Theil indices, Lorenz curves and the Palma ratio.

  6. Stage 6

    Hospital layer

    Service level, emergency room, ownership, PhilHealth accreditation, Malasakit Center and cost class for each facility.

  7. Stage 7

    Bed tracking

    E-mail agents collect free beds and report age, tested in a simulated mail environment.

  8. Stage 8

    The app

    Routing, fares, access maps and hospital mode in one web application.

Privacy policy

What this site collects

Nothing. This website has no sign-up, no forms that send data, no analytics and no tracking. The fare calculator runs in your browser and keeps nothing.

Third-party services

The typefaces load from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address and browser details under Google's own privacy policy. The service hosting this page may keep standard server logs for security.

The app

The MAP-A app sets no cookies and has no analytics. To draw the map it fetches tiles, routes and place names from Esri, OpenStreetMap, OSRM and Nominatim, which receive your IP address as with any web request. Pin corrections you make are kept only in your own browser. Patient status and payment choices in hospital mode are used for the current search only and are not kept.

Installing MAP-A

If you install MAP-A, your browser keeps a copy of the app and of this site on your device so they open without a signal. The copy stays on your device. Uninstalling MAP-A or clearing the site's data deletes it.

Your rights

The Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) gives you rights over personal data about you. This site keeps no personal data, so there is nothing stored here to access, correct or delete.
