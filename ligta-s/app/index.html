Who goes where
Origins (barangays or puroks) editable

Zones: space-separated tags such as PDZ, EDZ or LAHAR. An origin evacuates when one of its tags is active in the scenario. Vulnerable: seniors 60 and over, children under five, persons with disability and pregnant women.

Shelters editable

Capacity is covered floor area over 3.5 m² per person (Sphere). Classrooms hold 18 each (7 m × 9 m). Accessible: vulnerable evacuees are placed only in accessible shelters.

Clearance time

The exact minute by which everyone in the school-preserving plan can be inside a shelter, from a maximum flow on the time-expanded road network. Close bridges to see what a lahar does.

Why classrooms come last

Republic Act 10821 (2016, Children's Emergency Relief and Protection Act): schools may serve as evacuation centers only when no other suitable space exists. DepEd guidance: use gyms and open spaces before classrooms, and limit the use to 15 days for medium or long-term displacement unless the LGU documents the need. Republic Act 12076 (Ligtas Pinoy Centers Act, 2024) provides for an evacuation center in every city and municipality.

LIGTA-S

Say it like ligtas, "LIG-tas": safe.

The app of the study Safe Shelter, Open Schools: A Verified Network-Flow Model for Planning Evacuations in Albay by Hazard Zone, Shelter Capacity, and Vulnerability While Keeping Classrooms Open.

Not an official order

LIGTA-S is a planning aid from a student research project. It is not an evacuation order, not an official plan of PHIVOLCS, the OCD, DepEd or any LGU, and not medical advice. In an emergency follow your barangay officials and the MDRRMO, and call 911.

Synthetic test network

The demo data is test network E1, a synthetic volcano built for the paper. Its places, roads, schools and numbers are made up to test the method; they are not Albay data.

Privacy

LIGTA-S has no account, no analytics and no tracking, and sends nothing anywhere. Your edits stay in this browser. Clearing the site's data deletes them.
