Why it matters

When Mayon stirs, classrooms fill up

Albay has 18 cities and municipalities, 720 barangays and 1,374,768 residents (PSA 2020 census). In the June 2023 Mayon unrest, more than 19,000 people from 26 barangays stayed in 26 evacuation centers (ReliefWeb, 2023), and in 2026 schools became shelters again.

Jan 6, 2026

PHIVOLCS raised Mayon Volcano to Alert Level 3, and Albay ordered the preemptive evacuation of the 6-km Permanent Danger Zone.

Interaksyon / Philstar, January 6, 2026
608 and 3,424

In January 2026, 608 learners were displaced from the danger zone, and 3,424 learners were affected because four schools became evacuation centers. Some schools shifted to modular or blended learning.

Philippine Daily Inquirer, 2026
34 classrooms

In May 2026, 34 classrooms in three Camalig schools sheltered 488 families, about 1,315 people. Ashfall reached up to 132 schools.

Daily Tribune, May 4, 2026; Philippine Star, May 6, 2026
RA 10821

The Children's Emergency Relief and Protection Act (2016): schools may be evacuation centers only when no other suitable space exists. DepEd guidance: gyms and open spaces before classrooms, and at most 15 days for medium or long-term displacement unless the LGU documents the need.

Republic Act No. 10821 and DepEd guidance
RA 12076

The Ligtas Pinoy Centers Act, signed December 6, 2024, provides for an evacuation center in every city and municipality. Where should each one go?

Republic Act No. 12076
3.5 m²

Covered living space per person in the Sphere Handbook (2018). A DepEd standard classroom, 7 m by 9 m = 63 m², therefore shelters 18 evacuees.

Sphere Handbook, 2018; DepEd classroom standard

How many classrooms does an evacuation really need, how long until everyone is safe, and where should new centers be built? LIGTA-S answers all three with mathematics that is proven, then checked.

Features

One app for the planner and the resident

Every screen below is the working LIGTA-S app, shown on the synthetic test network E1 that comes with it. Replace the demo with your own barangays, shelters and roads.

Planner mode

Who goes where, with classrooms last

Pick a scenario and LIGTA-S finds the exact plan in the order the law sets: the most vulnerable residents placed, then the most residents placed, then the fewest in classrooms, the fewest in school gyms and covered courts, and the least travel.

  • Alert Level 3, Alert Level 4 and a compound lahar scenario with closed bridges
  • Editable tables of origins (population, vulnerable residents) and shelters (floor areas, classrooms, accessibility)
  • Persons per tier, classrooms closed (packed 18 per room), learners displaced and classroom-days for any length of stay
  • The time-only plan and the nearest-site rule side by side, so the classrooms saved and the extra minutes are plain

Test network E1 (synthetic), Alert Level 3.

Clearance time

The exact minute everyone is safe

Roads carry only so many people per minute. LIGTA-S expands the road map over time and solves maximum flows to find the first minute by which every evacuee of the plan can be inside a shelter, not an estimate.

  • Clearance time by doubling and bisection, with a quick lower bound beside it
  • Tap a bridge to close it, or close every bridge over a lahar channel at once
  • See how many minutes a closure adds, and who is cut off from every shelter

Test network E1 (synthetic), Alert Level 3.

Ligtas Pinoy Center siting

Where the next evacuation center should go

For each set of candidate sites, LIGTA-S counts how many more evacuees could be sheltered outside classrooms within a travel-time standard. It picks sites greedily, a rule proven to reach at least 63.2% of the best possible set, and checks the choice against every set.

  • Candidate sites with floor areas, a 30-minute standard by default, any number of sites
  • The plan recomputed with the new centers: classrooms, learners and clearance time

Test network E1 (synthetic), Alert Level 4 with lahar.

Resident mode and tools

“Where do I go?” in one tap

Residents choose their barangay and see their assigned shelter at each alert level, the trip time, and whether the shelter is accessible, with a switch for households that include a senior, a small child, a person with disability or a pregnant woman.

  • Capacity calculator: floor area to persons at 3.5 m² each, classrooms to evacuees at 18 per room
  • Edits saved on the device; export and import as JSON or CSV
  • Works offline once installed

Test network E1 (synthetic). A planning aid, not an evacuation order.

Mathematics and Computational Sciences

How MCS comes into play

The road map is a graph G = (N, E): each road e takes τe minutes and carries at most ue evacuees per minute. Shelters split into three tiers: non-school space, school open space (gyms and covered courts), and classrooms. The rest is mathematics, computing and science working together.

MathematicsA lexicographic minimum-cost flow, with ten propositions proven

The legal order of priorities becomes one objective, compared level by level. Penalty weights turn it into a single minimum-cost flow, and because that network is totally unimodular, every plan comes out in whole persons.

Ten propositions are proven, among them: packing classrooms full is nearly optimal, a higher alert level never frees classrooms, and greedy siting is near-optimal.

ComputingExact algorithms small enough for a phone

In the app each trip costs a vector, compared entry by entry, so no huge penalty number can lose precision. The time-expanded network turns “how fast can everyone get out?” into maximum flows.

  • Two maximum flows (Dinic) fix how many vulnerable residents and how many residents in all can be placed.
  • Successive shortest paths with a queue-based Bellman-Ford on vector costs then find the exact lexicographic plan.
  • Doubling and bisection find the clearance time with about 2 log2 T* maximum flows.

SciencesHazard zones, space standards and the law

Volcanology and hydrology decide who must move: the 6-km Permanent Danger Zone, an extended zone, and lahar channels whose bridges fail together. Humanitarian standards decide how many fit: 3.5 m² per person, so 18 per classroom. The law decides the order: RA 10821 puts classrooms last.

The research

Read the paper

Safe Shelter, Open Schools: A Verified Network-Flow Model for Planning Evacuations in Albay by Hazard Zone, Shelter Capacity, and Vulnerability While Keeping Classrooms Open

Brimbuela, Peter John A.; De Asis, Dave Justin Ll.; Shin, Yeaseong. Adviser: Calleja, Dick Anthony M. Legazpi City Science High School. Mathematics and Computational Sciences, Team (MCS-T), 2026.

A laptop-solvable network model built from open data sends every evacuee in a hazard zone to a reachable shelter with room, vulnerable residents first, then everyone, then the fewest persons in classrooms, the fewest in school open spaces, and the least travel. A time-expanded network gives the clearance time with and without lahar-damaged bridges, and a coverage function locates the Ligtas Pinoy Centers required by Republic Act No. 12076. Ten results were proven, then checked on synthetic instances.

Policies

Disclaimer and privacy

A planning aid, not an order

LIGTA-S is a student research project. It is not an evacuation order, not an official plan of PHIVOLCS, the Office of Civil Defense, DepEd or any local government, and not medical advice.

In an emergency, follow your barangay officials and the MDRRMO, and call 911.

Synthetic test network

Every number on this site about plans, classrooms, clearance times or sites comes from test network E1, a synthetic volcano built to test the method. It is not Albay data. Facts about Mayon, Albay and the law are cited with their sources.

Results for real places are only as good as the shelter, road and population data entered.

Privacy

LIGTA-S has no account, no analytics and no tracking. The app makes no network requests: your edits stay in your browser's storage on your device, and clearing the site's data deletes them.

This website loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address; the app uses only the fonts on your device. If you install LIGTA-S, your browser keeps a copy of the app and this site so they open offline.
