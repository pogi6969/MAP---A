SPIKE Line Coder

Pybricks code for a SPIKE Prime line follower: from a line map, from calibration, or from your choices at each junction.

Robot setup (used by all 3 generators)

Upload a line map

Click the map on the line where the robot starts, then click once more a bit ahead to set its direction. A sample track is loaded.

Route

Click a junction to choose S (straight), L or R.

What the code does

  1. PD line following: turn = Kp·error + Kd·Δerror, error from the reflection values.
  2. Speed from the map: fast on straights, slow before curves (the map's curvature at each distance).
  3. At each junction (both sensors on black) it takes the choice you set, then finds the line again. Gaps: drives straight until it sees the line.



Calibrate sensors


  1. Run this program. Put the sensors over white and press right.
  2. Put them over the black line and press left.
  3. Copy the CAL line from the console and paste it here.







Teach & replay


  1. First run: it follows the line. At every junction it stops and waits: left = turn left, right = turn right, Bluetooth = straight. Each choice is saved in the hub.
  2. Hold left + right together at a junction to finish and save.
  3. Next runs: no stopping. It uses the saved choices at full speed.
  4. Hold left while starting the program to teach again.
