Husky Maze Coder

Upload a top-down picture of your maze. The app finds the black walls, plans the shortest path from the start to the goal and writes the micromouse program with your map built in. The robot drives that path, checks every wall with its ultrasonic sensors and camera, and plans a new route around anything that blocks it.
